# Glossary

**Task**: A single thing to do. Has a title, may have a due date (date only) and a note, and is either open or done. Always belongs to exactly one List.

**List**: A named group of Tasks (e.g. "Work", "Home"). A default List called "Inbox" always exists at the start.

**Done**: The state of a Task the owner has ticked off. Done Tasks are kept, shown apart from open Tasks, and can later be deleted permanently.

**Overdue**: An open Task whose due date is before today.

**Passphrase**: The single shared secret the owner enters once per device to gain access to their Tasks.
